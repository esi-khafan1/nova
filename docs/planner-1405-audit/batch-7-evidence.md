### How the offset was determined

Printed page numbers were read from the footers of many pages of each
book (bottom-of-page digit blocks extracted with PyMuPDF) and matched
against PDF page numbers; the modal difference is the offset, cross-checked
against the imprint page and the front TOC. Counts of footer confirmations:
61: 164; 62: 151; 63: 142; 64: 143 (last numbered page 143; PDF p.152 is
unnumbered); 65: 106; 66: 70; 67: 108; 68: 179; 69: 126; 70: 122 (last
numbered page 123; the appendix pages after it carry no footers).

### How coverage was verified

1. The seed bank was parsed from the related migrations (195: 417 topics +
   125 fixes; 202: 242 topics + 28 fixes; 174: 77 topics + 14 fixes;
   187: 159 topics + 59 fixes) and from the v1 migrations (132/135/137).
2. For each book, every seed title was checked against the extracted PDF
   text with token-set matching over two-line windows (Persian text
   extraction reverses word order inside RTL lines and splits words on
   ZWNJ, so substring matching is unreliable; the matcher normalises
   ة→ه, ي→ی, ك→ک and Persian/Arabic digits→Latin). Every flag the matcher
   raised was then resolved by reading the actual page.
3. Heading spans were extracted by font/size/colour to enumerate every
   تمرین / فعالیت / کار در کلاس / خواندنی block and its page, and the
   تمرین blocks' ends were determined by reading the following pages.
4. A PGlite probe applied the full dependency chain (128, 132, 133, 134,
   135, 137, 167, 174, 187, 192, 195, 202) and counted NULL-page rows for
   the ten subjects: **zero**. It also confirmed no prior row uses
   position ≥ 3000 for any of these (subject, chapter) combos, so the new
   positions cannot collide.
5. Back matter was checked page by page (منابع, واژه‌نامه, اعتبارسنجی
   teacher lists, نظرسنجی letters, فصل/lesson dividers, blank pages).

### Front/back-matter policy (all books)

Front matter (پیشگفتار, فهرست), فصل/lesson divider and blank pages get no
rows. Back matter (منابع, واژه‌نامه) gets a row under the last chapter when
its printed pages are verified, as in audit batches 4–5. نظرسنجی letters
and اعتبارسنجی teacher lists get no rows. فعالیت / کار در کلاس / خواندنی
boxes fall inside their lesson/subtopic rows and are not separate rows
(batch-4 precedent: هندسه ۱); distinct end-of-lesson تمرین blocks DO get
their own rows titled «تمرین <lesson/subtopic>» (batch-4 حسابان/گسسته,
batch-1 آمار و احتمال). Overlapping ranges are allowed where two items
genuinely share a page.
